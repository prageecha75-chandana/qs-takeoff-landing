§ 01 · The Problem

QS work isn't generic measurement.

Generic takeoff tools were built for general drafting and markup. They don't understand BOQ structure, schedule-driven openings, or the way QSs actually price work.

[Product] is built around how Quantity Surveyors think.

Groups follow SLS 573 trades. Openings deduct automatically from their host walls. Exports come BOQ-ready with sections, sub-types, and totals already laid out.

Generic markup tools Status quo

  • 01Flat groups. You sort trades manually in Excel afterwards.
  • 02Openings are measured separately and forgotten at deduction time.
  • 03Exports are CSV dumps — no sections, no sub-totals.
  • 04Built for site engineers and designers, not for pricing work.

[Product] QS-native

  • 01Groups map to SLS 573 trades from the moment you create them.
  • 02Schedule-driven cutouts auto-deduct from their host walls.

§ 02 · Capabilities

Four things only a QS tool does right.

F · 01AI-assisted

AI Group Detection

Identify measurement groups across an entire drawing set in seconds. Cross-check against your manual takeoff to catch missed items before they become RFIs.

F · 02Deductions

Schedule-driven cutouts

Define door and window schedules once. Auto-deduct openings from wall measurements with width, height, and sill data flowing through to the BOQ.

F · 03Standards

SLS 573-aware grouping

Groups map to standard method of measurement trades from the start. No re-sorting at export time, no copy-paste into Excel.

F · 04Export

BOQ-ready export

Excel, CSV, and PDF outputs structured for direct use in CIDA SBD contract documents. Summary and detail sheets included.

§ 03 · The Workflow

Three steps from drawing to BOQ.

01·import

Import.

Drop in your PDF drawing set. Calibrate scale once per page. Multi-page support for full project sets.

02·measure

Measure.

Set up measurement groups by trade. Draw walls, slabs, doors, fittings. Wall thickness renders to scale on the canvas so you see exactly what you've measured.

03·export

Export.

One click for a complete BOQ in Excel or PDF. Data integrity warnings catch missing dimensions before they reach the contract document.
